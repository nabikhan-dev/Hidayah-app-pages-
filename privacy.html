The short version

  • No accounts: Hidayah has no sign-up or login and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • No ads or tracking: there are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Your app data stays on your device: tasbeeh activity, goals, settings, preferences, and bookmarks are stored locally and are not received by us.
  • Optional features use limited data: location and voice features operate only when you choose to use them. Some requests are handled by the external services listed below.

1. Introduction, scope, and publisher

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hidayah mobile application for iOS and Android, package or bundle identifier com.nabi12.NoorTasbeeh (the “App”), and this informational website.

The App is published by Stratix Solutions (“Hidayah,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy describes the App's current data practices as of the date shown above.

This website contains no account system, contact form, advertising, analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. Once you deploy it, your hosting provider may process ordinary web-server information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own terms. For details, consult the privacy policy of the service hosting this website.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or maintain a user profile or database of Hidayah users. In particular, we do not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or account credentials;
  • payment or purchase informationthe App has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid plans;
  • advertising identifiers or data used for targeted advertising;
  • analytics, engagement profiles, or cross-app and cross-site tracking data;
  • crash reports or diagnostics through a crash-reporting SDK;
  • your stored tasbeeh history, goals, settings, madhhab or maslak choice, language, chosen reciter, or bookmarks; or
  • microphone recordings.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal information. We do not operate servers that receive your locally stored Hidayah data.

3. Information processed on your device

Hidayah stores the following information only in local storage on your device so the App can remember your choices:

  • tasbeeh counts, saved history, and goals;
  • app settings, language, and madhhab or maslak selection;
  • your chosen Quran reciter; and
  • Quran bookmarks.

This local data does not leave your device and is never received by us. It remains available only on that device until you delete it through the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Because we do not receive it, we cannot view it, recover it, transfer it to another device, or restore it after deletion.

4. Device permissions and network access

Hidayah requests a permission only when it supports a relevant feature. Location, microphone and speech recognition, and notifications are optional. If you deny or revoke a permission, the related feature may be unavailable or less accurate, but other parts of the App remain usable.

Location while using the App

Why: Hidayah uses precise or approximate location while the App is open to calculate prayer times, determine Qibla direction, and find nearby mosques. Android permissions are ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. On iOS, access is limited to “When In Use.” Background location is explicitly blocked.

What leaves the device: coordinates are sent only to the relevant prayer-time and map services identified in Section 5 so they can return the requested results. We operate no servers, do not store those coordinates, and do not link them to an identity.

Your choice: location permission is optional. You can choose a location manually instead.

How to revoke: on iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Hidayah and choose Never. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Hidayah → Permissions → Location and choose Don't allow. Menu names may differ slightly by device and operating-system version.

Microphone and speech recognition

Why: these permissions are used only in the Quran recitation-practice feature and only after you actively start a practice session. Speech recognition helps compare your spoken recitation with the verse text.

How processing works: recognition may happen on your device or may be processed by Apple or Google platform speech-recognition services, depending on your device, operating-system configuration, language, and platform availability. Any platform processing is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

What we store: we do not store or upload microphone recordings, and we do not receive them.

How to revoke: on iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition, then disable Hidayah. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Hidayah → Permissions → Microphone and choose Don't allow. You can continue using features that do not require voice input.

Notifications

Why: Hidayah uses local device notifications for prayer times and reminders only. These alerts are scheduled on your device; we do not operate a push-notification server.

How to revoke: disable reminders inside Hidayah or, on iOS, open Settings → Notifications → Hidayah and turn off Allow Notifications. On Android, open Settings → Notifications → App notifications → Hidayah and turn notifications off. Menu names may differ by device.

Internet access

Why: an internet connection is used to fetch Quran text, translations, recitation audio, prayer times, Hijri dates, map tiles and nearby-mosque data, and App updates. Opening Sunnah.com or Quran.com references also launches your browser.

Your choice: you may restrict network access using your device settings, firewall, or mobile-data controls. Online content and services will not load while access is blocked, but locally available features may continue to work.

6. Data retention and deletion

We have no server-side user database and therefore retain no Hidayah account or activity data. Local data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete relevant history within the App where a delete or reset option is available, clear Hidayah's app storage through your device settings, or uninstall Hidayah. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes Hidayah's locally stored data from that device.

We cannot recover data after it is deleted or the App is uninstalled. Data processed independently by a third-party provider is retained according to that provider's own policy; contact the provider directly about its records or rights process.

7. Children's privacy

Hidayah is suitable for a general audience and can be used by people of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or the equivalent minimum age in their country. Hidayah has no accounts, advertising, behavioral tracking, or direct collection of contact information.

Optional location and recitation-practice features can cause information to be processed by a device platform or third-party service solely to return the requested result. Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use of these optional permissions and external links and review the relevant provider policies. If you believe we have received a child's personal informationwhich should not occur under the App's designplease contact us so we can investigate.

8. Your legal rights

European Economic Area and UK GDPR / UK GDPR

To the extent data protection law applies, the legal basis for optional location, microphone, and speech-recognition access is your consent, expressed through the platform permission prompt and your decision to start the relevant feature. Requests to external services are also initiated to perform the feature you ask Hidayah to provide. You can withdraw permission at any time in device settings without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing; receive portable data; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Because we do not receive or control your locally stored Hidayah data, we generally have no copy to access, correct, export, or erase. You exercise control by changing settings or deleting the local data. For information processed independently by a listed provider, direct your request to that provider.

California CCPA / CPRA

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that trigger a right to limit under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not offer financial incentives for personal information.

California residents may have rights to know, access, correct, and delete personal information and to receive equal service when exercising those rights. Because we do not maintain user accounts or receive locally stored app data, we generally have no user record to provide or delete. Third-party providers control their own records and requests.

Other regions

You may have similar privacy rights under the law where you live. Contact us with a privacy question, or contact the relevant third-party provider regarding information it handles. We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

9. Security

Hidayah minimizes risk by not creating accounts, not operating user-data servers, and storing personal app activity locally. Device-level protectionssuch as your passcode, biometric lock, operating-system security, backups, and access by other people or appsremain under your control.

No method of device storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Requests to third-party services travel over network connections and are subject to the security practices of your device, network, platform, and the receiving provider.

10. International users

Hidayah can be used in different countries. We do not transfer locally stored Hidayah data to our own servers because we do not operate such servers. When you request online content, mapping, location-based results, updates, or platform speech recognition, the relevant provider may process the request in a country different from yours. Review that provider's policy for information about international transfers and safeguards.

11. Apple App Store and Google Play disclosures

This policy is intended to remain consistent with Hidayah's Apple App Store privacy-label and Google Play Data Safety disclosures: no accounts, contact-data collection, advertising, analytics, tracking, data sale, or server-side storage by the publisher; local preferences and activity stay on the device; and optional location and voice-related processing is used only for app functionality as described above.

Apple and Google use their own definitions for “collection,” “sharing,” and on-device or off-device processing. Because coordinates are transmitted to external prayer-time and map services and platform speech recognition may process voice input, the store forms must disclose these flows according to the current platform questions and the final production build. The policy and store forms should be reviewed together after every SDK, API, or feature change.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Hidayah's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide notice in the App or through another appropriate method where practical.
