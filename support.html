Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my tasbeeh counter or history?

Use the reset control on the tasbeeh counter screen to reset the current count. To remove saved entries, open the tasbeeh history and use its clear or delete action, then confirm. Resetting or deleting cannot be undone.

How do I change my location?

Open Hidayah's location or prayer settings and choose a location manually. If you prefer automatic location, allow location access while using the App. On iOS, use Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Hidayah. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Hidayah → Permissions → Location. Menu names may vary by device.

How do I turn off prayer-time notifications?

Turn prayer reminders off in Hidayah's notification settings. You can also disable all Hidayah notifications in iOS under Settings → Notifications → Hidayah, or in Android under Settings → Notifications → App notifications → Hidayah.

How do I delete all Hidayah data?

Uninstall Hidayah from your device. Your tasbeeh counts, history, goals, settings, madhhab or maslak choice, language, chosen reciter, and bookmarks are stored only in local device storage, so uninstalling permanently deletes them from that device. We cannot recover the data afterward.

Can I use Hidayah without location permission?

Yes. Location access is optional. Choose your location manually for location-based features. Automatic prayer-time, Qibla, and nearby-mosque results may require a selected location and an internet connection.

Why might prayer times or the Hijri date differ locally?

Results can vary by calculation method, madhhab or juristic setting, location, time zone, and local moon sighting. Verify important dates and prayer times with your local mosque or a qualified scholar.

Does Hidayah record my recitation?

Hidayah does not store or upload recordings. Microphone and speech recognition are used only after you start a recitation-practice session. Recognition may occur on-device or through Apple or Google platform services under their own privacy policies.

Is Hidayah really free?

Yes. Hidayah is free and has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
